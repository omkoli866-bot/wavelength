Community Guidelines

Last updated August 2026

You must be 18 or older to use Wavelength. This platform is not intended for minors.

The Basics

  • Treat the person on the other end with basic human respect.
  • Do not share personal information (names, phone numbers, addresses).
  • No harassment, hate speech, threats, or solicitation of any kind.
  • No illegal activity, spam, or scams.

Staying Safe

Use the Skip or Leave buttons whenever you want to end a conversation. Use Report to flag abuse.
