Community guidelines

Last updated August 2026

You must be 18 or older to use Wavelength. If you're under 18, please leave — this platform isn't built or moderated with minors in mind.

The basics

  • Be a person you'd be comfortable being. Assume the stranger on the other end is a real person having a real day.
  • Don't share anyone's personal information — yours or anyone else's — including full names, addresses, phone numbers, or financial details.
  • No harassment, hate speech, threats, or targeted abuse of any kind.
  • No sexual content, solicitation, or content involving minors. This applies even in text-only conversations.
  • No illegal activity: no drugs, weapons, scams, or content that breaks the law where you or your chat partner live.
  • No spam, advertising, or trying to move the conversation to another platform for commercial purposes.

What "anonymous" actually means here

You don't need an account to chat, and messages aren't saved after a conversation ends. That's not the same as untraceable — reported conversations may be reviewed, and this platform can still cooperate with legal requests where required.

If something goes wrong

Use the Report button in any chat to flag a conversation and leave immediately. You don't owe anyone an explanation for ending a chat — the Skip and Leave buttons exist so you're never stuck.

Enforcement

Accounts or devices tied to repeated reports, harassment, or illegal content may be blocked from the platform. Because this is a lightweight anonymous service, moderation here is reactive rather than real-time — treat every chat with the same caution you'd use talking to someone you just met in public.
